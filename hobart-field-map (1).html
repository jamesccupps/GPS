Averaging is how you make a control point. Stand still, phone flat and level in an open palm, body clear of it, and let it run the full 60 seconds. Random error averages down; a single reading is the worst number your phone can give you.

Your plan geometry is exact — the deed traverse closes at 1:238,800. Only its placement on the earth floats, by about ±10–20 ft, because it was fitted to the road centerline.

So you don't need a survey rover. Find one monument, average on it, and shift the whole parcel to match. Everything else comes along at plan accuracy.
