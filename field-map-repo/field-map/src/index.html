Bearings everywhere in this app are grid north, matching your plan. The phone's magnetometer reads magnetic north, about 14.6° west of true here. That is corrected automatically — but if you walk a deed bearing with a handheld compass, you must add it yourself.

Your plan geometry is exact — the deed traverse closes at 1:238,800. Only its placement floats, about ±10–20 ft, because it was fitted to the road centerline. Find one monument, average on it, and shift the whole parcel to match.
